Circuit First Light

Trace the supply and return. Turn a wire, choose a resistor value, and bring the circuit to life.

An Android circuit puzzle by AtwoL, with 100 handmade levels across 15 chapters. Core puzzles work offline. The game includes optional sound, hints, Undo and three visual skins.

The ad-supported version places banners on chapter/level selection and completed-level results. There are no advertisements during puzzle play or circuit review, and ad availability never gates progress.

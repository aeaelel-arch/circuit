Circuit First Light

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

AtwoL provides Circuit First Light, a free Android circuit puzzle game. This policy explains how the game and its support website handle information. Contact aeaelel@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

1. Information stored on your device

The game stores puzzle completion, selected level, personal best moves and scores, guide acknowledgements, sound and appearance preferences, and a coarse age-group choice on your device. It does not require a player account or a developer-operated gameplay server. The game does not send your puzzle saves or age-group selection to AtwoL.

The age screen does not ask for a name, exact birth date or identity document. You can correct your age group under Settings → Change age range. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling removes its local information; device-managed restoration may depend on Android settings. AtwoL does not provide a cloud account from which these saves can be recovered.

2. Advertising and Google services

The ad-supported game uses Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) for advertising and applicable privacy choices. Banners may appear on chapter/level selection and completed-level results. They do not appear during puzzle play, circuit review, guides or settings. The core puzzles remain playable when advertising cannot load.

Advertising and consent services communicate with Google over the internet. Depending on the service, device, region and request settings, Google may process network information such as an IP address and approximate location inferred from it, device/app information and identifiers, app and advertisement interactions, and diagnostic information. These services use information for advertising delivery and measurement, fraud prevention and service operation. Nonpersonalized advertising does not mean that no information is processed.

The game configures child-directed treatment, a G-rated advertisement content limit and nonpersonalized requests for all players. The current app does not request Android advertising-ID access or precise device location. These restrictions do not eliminate Google’s other advertising, privacy or security processing.

See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps for details about Google’s processing. AtwoL does not operate a separate gameplay analytics service or sell your local puzzle saves.

3. Age groups and children

Circuit is intended for children and older players. A neutral age-group question appears on first use, with an option not to specify an age. The coarse choice stays in a separate local preferences file. All choices currently receive the same conservative advertising treatment described above; an age choice is not consent to advertising data processing.

AtwoL does not ask children to submit personal contact details to play. If a parent or guardian believes a child has sent personal information to AtwoL, contact aeaelel@gmail.com so the message can be reviewed and appropriate action taken.

4. Privacy choices

Google’s consent service manages applicable advertising privacy information and choices. Where required and applicable to the user’s age/region, available privacy messages and options depend on that service’s eligibility and configuration. When the SDK requires a privacy-options entry point, the game provides Privacy choices on the Levels or Results page. Changing an age group does not itself grant advertising consent.

Advertising availability or privacy choices never change access to earned progress, hints or the free levels. Android may also provide device-level advertising controls.

5. Support messages

If you contact AtwoL by email, AtwoL receives your email address, message and information you choose to include. This is used to answer the request, provide support and handle related privacy inquiries. Email is handled through Gmail, so Google also processes messages under its privacy terms. Support messages are retained while needed for the request and necessary follow-up, or as required by law. You may request deletion by emailing the same address.

6. Retention and deletion

Local game information remains on the device until you remove it or the app replaces it during normal use. To remove it, use Android’s app-storage controls or uninstall the game. Removing local information resets progress and preferences; changing an age group alone does not erase puzzle progress.

Google’s retention and deletion practices are separate from AtwoL’s local game saves and support inbox. Uninstalling Circuit does not promise deletion of information already held by Google. Refer to Google’s retention information and its privacy controls for provider-held information.

7. Security and other destinations

Local information uses Android’s app storage. Google documents encryption in transit for information sent by its advertising SDK. AtwoL uses the information described here for the stated purposes; no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed absolutely secure.

If you open an advertisement or another external link, the destination may be operated by another provider and have its own privacy practices.

8. This website

These support pages contain no custom analytics, advertisement scripts, forms or tracking cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the support website and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, even without a GitHub sign-in. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

9. Policy updates and contact

AtwoL may update this policy when the app’s practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to aeaelel@gmail.com.
